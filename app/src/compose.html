Write it for me

  1. Type what you want, like “reply saying Friday works, many thanks”.
  2. Press Ctrl+Alt+W. The text streams in where your instruction was.
  3. It reads what's around your cursor and on screen (the chat, the email, the document) to get the context. You can also copy something for it to use.
Speed

Typo fixing never uses any of this and stays fully offline. Nothing is read or sent until you press the hotkey. Copied text is only used if you copied it in the last 15 minutes, and never if it looks like a password or a link.
