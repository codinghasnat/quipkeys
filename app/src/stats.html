How is time saved worked out?

It's an estimate. Without QuipKeys, a wrong word costs you about half a second to notice, then backspacing over it and typing it again. QuipKeys times that at your typing speed. An undone fix doesn't count. For Write it for me, it's the time you'd have spent typing the text yourself, minus typing your instruction and waiting for it.

Only counts are kept, in quipkeys-stats.txt on this computer: never any words.
