Type fast. Stop fixing typos.

Each time you press space, QuipKeys checks the word you just typed and quietly fixes the slip.

You type
I wamt to gp to the stpre
You get
I want to go to the store

It fixes neighbouring keys, swapped letters, doubled or missing letters, and spaces in the wrong place. When it isn't sure what you meant, it leaves your word alone.

Private by design

A tool that sees your keyboard has to earn your trust. Here's exactly what QuipKeys does and doesn't do.

Everything runs on this computerThe dictionary and language model are built into the app. It works the same with no internet connection.
Nothing you type is keptIt only looks at the last few words, in memory, and forgets them when you press Enter, click or switch apps. The one thing it saves is your word list: words you teach it, and nothing else.
Nothing is sent anywhereNo account, no analytics, no tracking. QuipKeys never connects to the internet.
Open sourceEvery line of code is public, so anyone can check these promises. See the code

It also stays out of password fields, terminals, code editors and password managers.

Three keys to know

There's nothing to learn. Type the way you normally do.

space
Fixes the word you just typed. The correction appears as the space does.
⌫ Backspace
Undoes a fix if you press it straight after one, and adds the word to your word list, so it's never changed again.
Pauses or resumes QuipKeys anywhere, any time.

One permission

macOS asks you to approve any app that reads the keyboard. QuipKeys uses this permission only to see the keys you press and type its corrections, nothing else.
