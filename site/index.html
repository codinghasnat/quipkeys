Sound-alikes, sorted

The hardest typos are real words: spell-check can't see them. QuipKeys reads the words on both sides with a small trained model, and fixes the slip once the sentence makes it clear.

Can you meat me at the cafe?Can you meet me at the cafe?
I got a letter form the bank.I got a letter from the bank.
Their is a problem, and I am to tired.There is a problem, and I am too tired.
I can't wait to sea you this weekend.I can't wait to see you this weekend.
Please fill out the form and return it.Please fill out the form and return it.

On our hand-written test of sound-alike slips it fixed 17 of 32 by itself and changed none of 442 correct words. For the rest, press Ctrl+Alt+N (Ctrl+Option+N on Mac) straight after the word: it offers the likeliest fix first.

Spell it how it sounds

Switch on Spell by sound and QuipKeys also reads words spelt by ear, then lets the sentence pick between sound-alikes. It's built with dyslexic typists in mind, and it's free like everything else here. It's off by default, so ordinary typing works exactly as before.

Write it for me.

Type a few words, press Ctrl+Alt+W (Ctrl+Option+W on Mac), and the finished text streams in where you typed. It reads the room: the app you're in, the conversation on screen, the email you're answering. No copying, no switching to a chatbot tab.

  1. Chats stay short. Emails get a greeting and a sign-off. Documents keep their voice.
  2. Keep typing while it writes and it stops; the rest waits on your clipboard.
  3. Never reads password fields, banking or password apps.

Stays on your computer

  • Offline by defaultTypo fixing never touches the internet: the dictionary and language model are built in. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Only Write it for me with an online model, and update checks, ever go online, and both are off until you switch them on.
  • Forgets as you goIt only looks at your last few words, and forgets them on Enter, a click or an app switch.

Get QuipKeys

Windows and Mac. Free for personal and non-commercial use. Ready-made downloads come with the first release on GitHub, and from then on QuipKeys can update itself. Until then, build it from the source with Rust:

Windowsgit clone https://github.com/codinghasnat/quipkeys
cd quipkeys\app
cargo build --release
target\release\quipkeys.exe
Macgit clone https://github.com/codinghasnat/quipkeys
cd quipkeys/app
sh macos/make-app.sh --install
